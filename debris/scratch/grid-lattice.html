Enemy Design — Visual Options 3 & 4

Grid & Lattice

Two of the five alien directions, built out: The Occupation Grid, faceless mechanized gun-platforms with no pilot and no face, and The Lattice, crystalline energy growths that can pass for debris until they wake up. Same large/small pairing both times — a dumb drifter and an accurate hunter sharing one shape language. The hunter's actual attack routine (approach → lock-on → fire → cooldown) is identical either way; only the skin changes.

Option 3

The Occupation Grid

Xevious/Gradius-lineage: cold, angular, faceless machinery. No eyes, no limbs — just armor plating around a gun core. The large variant broadcasts its threat through sheer bulk; the small one strips that bulk away until it's nothing but a frame and a barrel.

Siege Platform

Slab armor, a slow-traversing gun ring that rotates on its own regardless of any target — armed, but not aiming. Trundles a straight line, same dumb drift as today's UFO.

Scout Turret

Armor stripped to an exposed strut frame around one oversized barrel. The barrel itself is the tell — it swivels to track, then flares at the muzzle on lock-on.

Approach — barrel tracking, dim

Lock-on — muzzle flaring, frame still

Fire — full flash, bolt released

Option 4

The Lattice

Phoenix/Defender-lineage: faceted crystal growth, no plating or limbs at all. The one option that can plausibly be mistaken for a drifting rock at a glance — the large variant stays dim and asleep on purpose; the small one is visibly awake, core blazing well past anything a real asteroid would do.

Geode

A rounded cluster of blunt facets around a dim, slow-pulsing core glimpsed through the gaps. Tumbles gently — inert cargo, easy to mistake for debris until it's already close.

Shard

A single elongated spike moving point-first, core blazing far brighter than the Geode's ever does — the "awake" tell is ambient, before lock-on even starts.

Approach — core bright, banking

Lock-on — tip flaring, body still

Fire — white flash, bolt released

The Occupation Grid introduces one new hue family (gunmetal armor, teal-cyan active accent — not reused from anywhere else in Debris). The Lattice introduces none: its crystal and glow both sit on the existing COLORS.blackHole violet already used for the Gravity Well, which is also the whole point of the option - it reads as something that could belong in this arena already.
