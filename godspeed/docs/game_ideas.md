# Game Ideas

## Setting

Ancient divine technology meets science fiction.

- Monolithic stone halls
- Floating geometric structures
- Massive statues
- Gold and obsidian architecture
- Glowing runes
- Dynamic lighting
- Echoing choirs with ominous synths

The world should constantly make players ask:

> Is this a temple… or a machine?

## Gameplay

- Procedural labyrinths
- Smarter enemy AI
- Permanent account unlocks
- Run-only upgrades
- Dynamic lighting
- Maze evolves during a run
